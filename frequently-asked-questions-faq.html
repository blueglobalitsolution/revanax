Skin Treatments
What skin treatments are available at ReevanaX?

We offer a comprehensive range of skin treatments including Laser Skin Tightening, PICO Laser, Anti-Aging Treatments, Skin Brightening, Acne Scar Removal, Stretch Mark Removal, Scar & Tattoo Removal, Mesotherapy, PRP Therapy, and more. Our skin specialists will recommend the best treatment based on your specific skin type and goals.

How many sessions are needed for laser skin treatment?

The number of sessions varies based on the treatment type, your skin condition, and your desired outcome. Generally, skin rejuvenation treatments require 3–6 sessions spaced 4 weeks apart for optimal results. Your specialist will give you a personalized treatment plan during your consultation.

Is bridal skin treatment available for weddings?

Yes! We offer specialized pre-wedding bridal skincare packages designed to give you a radiant, glowing complexion on your big day. Our bridal packages include facials, skin brightening, PRP, and customized treatments. We recommend starting 3–6 months before your wedding for best results.

Are skin treatments painful?

Most of our skin treatments are minimally invasive and well-tolerated. Some procedures may cause mild discomfort, tingling, or warmth during the treatment. Topical anaesthetic creams are applied before laser procedures to ensure your comfort throughout the session.

Hair Treatments
What hair loss treatments does ReevanaX offer?

We offer advanced hair loss solutions including Hair PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma), Hair Mesotherapy, Omega Light Therapy, Stepwise Baldness Treatment, Hair Transplant consultations, and customized programs for both men and women. Treatments are tailored to your hair loss stage and pattern.

Is hair PRP treatment effective?

Yes, Hair PRP is a clinically proven treatment that uses growth factors from your own blood to stimulate hair follicles and promote new hair growth. Most patients see significant improvement in hair density and thickness after 3–6 sessions. Results vary based on individual hair loss patterns.

Do you offer pre-wedding hair treatments for grooms?

Absolutely. We have dedicated pre-wedding grooming packages for grooms that include hair strengthening, scalp treatments, beard grooming, and skin treatments. We recommend starting at least 3 months before your wedding for the best results. Read our blog on groom's pre-wedding treatments to learn more.

Surgery & Procedures
What plastic surgery procedures are available?

We offer a range of plastic surgery procedures including Rhinoplasty, Blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery), Lip Surgery, Facelift (Rhytidectomy), Chin Augmentation, Breast Augmentation, Breast Reduction, Breast Lift, Tummy Tuck, Liposuction, Butt Lift, and more. All surgeries are performed by experienced certified surgeons.

How long is the recovery time after surgery?

Recovery time varies depending on the type of procedure. Minor procedures may have a recovery of a few days, while more complex surgeries can require 2–6 weeks. Your surgeon will provide a detailed post-care plan and follow-up schedule to ensure a smooth recovery.

Are surgical results permanent?

Many surgical results are long-lasting or permanent, particularly structural procedures like rhinoplasty, liposuction, and breast augmentation. However, factors like aging, weight changes, and lifestyle habits can affect results over time. Non-surgical treatments may require maintenance sessions.

What is gynec aesthetics and who is it for?

Gynec aesthetics refers to cosmetic procedures of the female intimate area performed for medical, functional, or aesthetic reasons. These include Labiaplasty, Vaginal Tightening, Vaginal Rejuvenation, Hymenoplasty, and more. These procedures are for women who desire improvement in comfort, appearance, or function. Strict privacy and confidentiality are maintained at all times.
