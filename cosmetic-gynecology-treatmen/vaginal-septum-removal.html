What is Vaginal Septum Removal?

Vaginal septum removal is a minor surgical procedure used to treat a congenital condition where a wall or "septum" divides the vaginal canal. This extra tissue can partially or completely block the vagina, leading to discomfort, difficulty with menstruation, pain during intercourse, or even complications during childbirth.

At ReevanaX Women's Clinic in Surat, we offer safe and effective treatment for vaginal septum through gentle, expert-led procedures—restoring both comfort and function.

Why You Might Need Vaginal Septum Removal

A vaginal septum is a condition present from birth and may remain undiagnosed until puberty or adulthood. You may benefit from this procedure if you experience:

  • Pain or difficulty during sexual activity
  • Menstrual obstruction or irregular flow
  • Difficulty inserting tampons or undergoing pelvic exams
  • Discomfort or complications during childbirth
  • A diagnosis of longitudinal or transverse vaginal septum

Types of Vaginal Septum Conditions Treated:

  1. Longitudinal Vaginal Septum:
    A vertical tissue wall dividing the vagina into two canals.
  2. Transverse Vaginal Septum:
    A horizontal tissue wall partially or fully blocking the vaginal canal.

Each case is unique, and the treatment approach is tailored to your anatomy and symptoms.

How Vaginal Septum Removal Works

During this outpatient procedure, the extra tissue is carefully excised using advanced surgical techniques to restore a single, functional vaginal canal. Healing is typically straightforward, with personalized aftercare instructions provided to ensure your comfort and well-being.
